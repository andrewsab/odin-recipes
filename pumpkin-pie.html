Pumpkin Pie

Description

This recipe is for a simple, but delicious pumpkin pie. With the fall season upon us, pumpkin pie is one of the of the best desserts to celebrate with for any event. Pair it with a glass of milk, or whip cream, pumpkin pie is a fall staple.

Ingredients

  • 1 Unbaked Pie Crust
  • 1 16oz can Pumpkin Puree
  • 1 14oz can Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 teaspoon Pumpkin Pie spice

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 Degrees F.
  2. Fit pie crust into a pie dish and place on baking sheet.
  3. Add pumpkin puree, condensed milk, eggs, and pumpkin pie spice into a large bowl and mix until combined.
  4. Pour mixture into pie crust and bake for 15 minutes. After 15 minutes, reduce heat to 350 degrees F, and continue to bake until filling is set, 35 to 40 minutes.
  5. Remove from oven, and let cool completely.
  6. Enjoy!
