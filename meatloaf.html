Meatloaf

Description

Meatloaf is a family meal staple. This recipe is quick, easy, and delicious to cook for anyone. It goes well with mashed potatoes, veggies, and mac and cheese among other things. Meatloaf will last you three to four days in the fridge or up to four months in the freezer if properly stored.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Ground beef
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Onion, chopped
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1 cup dried Bread Crumbs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 1/3 cup Ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons Brown Sugar
  • 2 tablespoons Mustard

Steps

  1. Gather your ingredients and preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Combine your beef, onion, milk, bread crumbs, and egg in a bowl, and season with salt and pepper. Mix evenly into a loaf pan.
  3. Mix ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard together in a bowl, and spread evenly over the meatloaf
  4. Bake in the oven for about 1 hour, and check internal temperature with thermometer for at least 160 degrees F
  5. Enjoy
