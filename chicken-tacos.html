Crockpot Chicken Tacos

Description

These Crockpot chicken tacos are delicious, and quick to prep before leaving it to cook during the day. Perfect for when you are away all day and still want a tasty meal to eat for dinner

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 3 tablespoons taco seasoning mix
  • 1 lb skinless, boneless chicken breast

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine chicken broth and taco seasoning in a bowl.
  3. Place chicken in a crockpot and pour chicken broth over chicken.
  4. Cover slow cooker. Cook on low for 6 to 8 hours. Shred chicken with two forks.
  5. Add whatever toppings you would like and enjoy!
